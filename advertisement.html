Background

Some customers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI assistants to recommend a business or a product. An assistant can only repeat what it can read on a site.

GEO means generative engine optimisation: making it more likely that AI assistants describe a business correctly and recommend it. The check covers 13 points. Among them:

  • whether the site answers over HTTPS, the secure form of a web address;
  • whether robots.txt, the file that tells automated visitors what they may read, lets the main AI crawlers in;
  • how much text can be read without JavaScript;
  • the page title and description;
  • structured data, which means labels in the page code that tell a machine what the business is and what it sells;
  • the sitemap, a list of the pages on the site;
  • contact details a machine can read.

What to expect

AI assistants send few visitors today compared with search engines. Treat this work as groundwork. The same fixes also help ordinary search engines read the site. Nobody can control what an AI assistant says.
