Background

A small business website is a few pages that say what the business does, where, at what price, and how to reach it.

Ours are static files. That means ordinary web pages stored as they are, with no database behind them.

Why it matters

In 2026, 24% of UK sole traders had no website (UK Business Data Survey 2026, Department for Science, Innovation and Technology with Ipsos, 4,450 businesses, published 18 June 2026).

A customer who cannot find opening hours, prices or a phone number on a site goes elsewhere.

Why choose us

  • The starting prices are fixed and are on this page.
  • The files are static, so there is no monthly platform fee from us, and you own the code.
  • The site works on phones.
  • It is built with HTTPS, a clear page for each service or product, structured data and contact details a machine can read. Structured data means labels in the page code that tell a machine what the business is and what it sells.
  • Delivery takes 3 to 7 days.
