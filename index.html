How a job works

The same five steps for every job. Figures for research papers have their own terms, set out on the Academic page.

  1. Email your website address for the free check, or say what you need.
  2. A fixed quote comes back within one working day.
  3. Half is paid before work starts and half on delivery, by card through a Stripe payment link.
  4. One round of changes is included.
  5. If the first delivery is not what was agreed and the changes do not fix it, the deposit is returned.
