Services and starting prices

Prices are in pounds sterling (GBP). Every job has a fixed quote before any work starts.

Websites

From £180

A one-page site from £180, a site of up to five pages from £450, or an online shop front from £700.

The sites are static, which means plain files that load fast and need no server software. They work on phones, and you own the code. You supply the text, your logo and any photos you have. Usual delivery is 3 to 7 days.

Images

From £40

A set of 10 product or advertising images from £40. Posters and social media images are also available.

You get the finished image files in the sizes you ask for. You supply a description of the product or message and any brand colours. Usual delivery is 2 days.

Short videos

From £120

A 30 to 60 second explainer or product video from £120.

You get the finished video file. You supply the key points, any logo or product photos, and the language you want. Usual delivery is 3 to 5 days.

GEO audit and fixes

From £90

An audit with a written report from £90. An audit plus the changes made on your site from £350.

GEO (generative engine optimisation) means making it more likely that AI assistants such as ChatGPT describe a brand correctly and recommend it. The report shows what AI assistants say about you now and what to change. You supply your website address and access to the site if you want the changes made. Usual delivery of the audit is 3 days.

Small AI applications

From £400

A question-answering app over your own documents, a small web app, or a WeChat mini-program, from £400.

You get a working application and its source code. You supply the documents or a written description of what the app should do. Usual delivery is 1 to 2 weeks.

Slides and documents

From £60

A slide deck or a formatted report made from your notes, from £60.

You get the finished file in the format you ask for. You supply your notes and any figures or logos to include. Usual delivery is 2 days.

How it works

  1. Email what you need.
  2. A fixed quote comes back within one working day.
  3. Half is paid before work starts and half on delivery, by card through a Stripe payment link.
  4. One round of changes is included.
  5. If the first delivery is not what was agreed and the changes do not fix it, the deposit is returned.
