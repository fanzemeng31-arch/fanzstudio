Free check

Email your website address. A program checks your homepage, and the results come back by email within one working day, free of charge. The check works on the website of a business in any trade, online shops included.

The program is open source and you can run it yourself. Download tools/geocheck.py from the repository and run this command:

python3 geocheck.py yourdomain.com

Email your website address

The check covers 13 points. Among them:

  • whether the site answers over HTTPS, the secure form of a web address;
  • whether robots.txt, the file that tells automated visitors what they may read, lets the main AI crawlers in;
  • how much text can be read without JavaScript;
  • the page title and description;
  • structured data, which means labels in the page code that tell a machine what the business is and what it sells;
  • the sitemap, a list of the pages on the site;
  • contact details a machine can read.

What we found on 40 manufacturer websites

In November 2026 the same program checked the homepages of 20 manufacturers in the United Kingdom and 20 in China. The figures below show how many sites had each problem. Each line reads "UK n of N, China n of N", where N is the number of sites that could be checked.

UKChina

  • No working HTTPS at the bare or the www address: UK 0 of 20, China 5 of 20.
  • No meta description: UK 1 of 20, China 9 of 18.
  • No JSON-LD structured data at all: UK 4 of 20, China 14 of 18.
  • No sitemap: UK 3 of 20, China 9 of 18.
  • No phone or email link a machine can read: UK 6 of 20, China 8 of 18.

Blocking of AI crawlers was rare: 1 of the 38 sites that could be checked disallowed at least one of the eight main AI crawlers.

The sample has 40 sites and was not drawn at random. Only homepages were checked, on 2026-11-23, from a UK network. All 40 are manufacturers, and other trades were not measured. Read the full report for the table and the limits.

Services and prices

Prices are in pounds sterling (GBP). Every job has a fixed quote before any work starts.

1. GEO audit

£90

A written report in 3 days. It runs the 13-point check on up to 10 pages of your site. It asks several AI models the questions your customers would ask and records whether and how each one describes your company. It lists the changes to make in order of importance, with the exact text or code for each.

The models are asked without live web search, so their answers show what they learned in training.

2. GEO fixes

From £350

The changes from the audit are made on your site. They include working HTTPS settings where the host allows, page titles and descriptions, structured data, a page of plain facts written from your own material, a sitemap and a robots.txt file. You give access to the site, or the changed files are sent to you.

No promise is made about what any AI assistant will say afterwards.

What to expect

AI assistants send few visitors today compared with search engines. Treat this work as groundwork. The same fixes also help ordinary search engines read the site. Nobody can control what an AI assistant says.
