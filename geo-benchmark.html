What a program found on 40 manufacturer websites

Some buyers ask AI assistants to name suppliers. An assistant can only describe a company whose website it can read. This report shows how well 40 manufacturer websites can be read by a program.

What was checked and why

The program geocheck.py (version 0.1) checked the homepage of each site on 2026-11-23. It looked at HTTPS, robots.txt, how much text is visible without JavaScript, the page description, structured data, the sitemap, llms.txt and contact details a machine can read. The aim was to see which basic problems are common.

The first two rows use all 40 sites as the base. Every other row uses only the sites that could be checked. A site counts as not checked when it did not answer, or when the internet provider of the network used for the test showed its own filter page in place of the site. A site that failed over HTTPS was tried again at its www address and then over plain HTTP.

How the sites were chosen

The 20 UK sites come from the public member directory of Made in Britain, engineering sector. The 20 Chinese sites come from the public exhibitor list of Valve World Asia 2026. Neither organisation took part in this work. No company is named here and no result for a single site is published.

Limits

  • The sample is small, with 40 sites. It was not drawn at random, so the figures describe these sites only.
  • Most Chinese sites are makers of valves and other flow-control equipment.
  • Only homepages were checked. Product pages may differ.
  • The "No Product type" row is about homepages only. A homepage does not usually carry product data, so 100% in that row does not show that the sites have no product data elsewhere.
  • HTTPS was tested from a UK network on 2026-11-23. Results from another network or another day may differ.

A note on llms.txt

llms.txt is a proposed convention. It is a plain text file that describes a site for AI assistants. AI vendors have not confirmed that they read it. The row is included because it is easy to check, and it should not be taken as a requirement.

Next steps

You can run the program on your own site: download tools/geocheck.py and run python3 geocheck.py yourdomain.com. Or email your website address for the free check. Back to the home page.
